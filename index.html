Privacy Policy for PorcuNote Cello

Last updated: October 2026

PorcuNote Cello respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information and device permissions.

Microphone Access

PorcuNote Cello uses microphone access solely to provide real-time pitch detection and tuning features.

Microphone audio is processed temporarily on your device. The app does not record, store, upload, transmit, or share microphone audio.

Local App Data

Certain preferences, such as language, reference pitch, tempo, meter, volume, display settings, and fingering-related settings, may be stored locally on your device.

These preferences are not transmitted to PorcuNote or to external servers.

Personal Data

PorcuNote Cello does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or personal files.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version of PorcuNote Cello does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party telemetry SDKs.

Payments

The current version of the app does not directly collect or store payment information. If in-app purchases are introduced, payments will be handled by the applicable app store payment system. PorcuNote Cello will not receive or store your full payment card information.

Third-Party and System Services

Android system services may independently provide system resources such as downloadable emoji fonts. These Android system services are separate from PorcuNote Cello and are not used by the app to collect microphone audio or personal information.

Children's Privacy

PorcuNote Cello does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's core features operate locally on the device without requiring an account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
